Blinkit turned adjusted EBITDA positive in December 2025 — ₹4 crore on ₹13,300 crore of net order value — while adding 211 stores and growing 121% year on year. Our DCF does not support this call and we say so: ₹238 against a ₹242.87 price, with 76% of value in terminal. Why we are long
  • Density deepens. Delhi NCR at ~3.5% and Gurgaon+Noida at ~5% of NOV prove a mature cluster earns real money. Consolidated Blinkit is at 0% because it is paying for 1,000 immature stores.
  • Throughput is holding. The network doubled without diluting NOV per store per day. That is the load-bearing fact.
  • Food delivery funds the land grab. Record margin, accelerating growth, negative capital employed, ₹18,911 crore of net cash after leases. No external funding required.
  • Negative working capital. 14 inventory days against 30 payable days — growth releases cash.
What breaks it
  • The fight lasts longer. Amazon Now, Flipkart Minutes, Reliance and an IPO-bound Zepto do not need this to be profitable. Blinkit need not lose — margins need only stay suppressed five more years.
  • Tier-2 dilutes. The next 2,000 stores may be structurally worse than the last 2,000.
  • Payable days compress. Most of the DCF's free cash flow disappears.
  • The DCF already disagrees at ₹238, and the multiple does all the work.
